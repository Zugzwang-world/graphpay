# ADR-0016: Parallel sessions with owned areas

Status: adopted from the lab (planning/DECISIONS.md part A) · Date: 2026-11-05 · Decided by: founder (pre-event) · Source: `planning/DECISIONS.md` part A, row 0016

## Decision

Parallel sessions: one worktree and branch each, owned areas, small PRs

## Why

Four to five sessions merged dozens of PRs in a night without stepping on each other
