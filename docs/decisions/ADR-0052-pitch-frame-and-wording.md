# ADR-0052: Pitch frame and wording

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-05 · Decided by: founder (brief v2)

## Decision

Identify (ENS), remember (Arkiv), pay (Whitechain): agents that can pay, can be trusted and can be stopped. Say "decentralized, secure and verifiable"; never "hidden". Readonly receipts "cannot be edited", never "permanent".

## Why

One frame everywhere: the website, the README, the sponsor files, the video and the pitch.

## Consequences

A rule in `CLAUDE.md`; M15 and M17 use it.
