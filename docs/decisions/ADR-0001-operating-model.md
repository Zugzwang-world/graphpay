# ADR-0001: Operating model

Status: adopted from the lab (planning/DECISIONS.md part A) · Date: 2026-11-05 · Decided by: founder (pre-event) · Source: `planning/DECISIONS.md` part A, row 0001

## Decision

Operating model: the founder speaks, the thinking partner plans, Claude Code runs whole steps unattended; one relay file; PRs auto-merge (squash) on green `ci` into a protected `main`; deny-list guardrails; a prompt-log hook; founder-only work goes to the operator queue (at the event: `docs/thinking-partner/OPEN.md`, F-4)

## Why

The founder works by voice and should only do what software can't; the relay is the only channel to the thinking partner
