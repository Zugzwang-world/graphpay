# ADR-0072: Mr. Vitalik is a fictional persona
Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-06 22:30 IST · Decided by: founder · Source: the full-sync brief `docs/thinking-partner/briefs/2225-full-sync.md`

## Decision
Mr. Vitalik, the customer in the story, is a fictional persona. He appears only as his caped smiley agent:
- no likeness of a real person;
- no endorsement lines;
- a fictional-persona note in the README.

## Why
The persona shares a name with a real person. Judges and visitors must not read the demo as that person's likeness or endorsement.

## Consequences
- The README carries the note. The Submission session repeats it in the submission text.
- The ENS names (`vitalik.graphpay.eth` and its agents) are labels in a test namespace on Sepolia. They are not an identity claim.
