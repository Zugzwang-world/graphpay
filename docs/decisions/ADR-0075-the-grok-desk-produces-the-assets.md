# ADR-0075: The Grok desk produces the assets, and its prompts stay in the repo
Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-06 22:30 IST · Decided by: founder · Source: the full-sync brief `docs/thinking-partner/briefs/2225-full-sync.md`; `assets/grok/`

## Decision
- **What it produces:** the founder's Grok desk produces the story's visual assets: the agents' figurines, the dish photos and the copy.
- **The proof:** its prompts and deliveries are kept in the repo (`assets/grok/part1`, `assets/grok/part2`). `.vercelignore` keeps them out of deployments.

## Why
The event's rules ask for AI use to be documented. The assets are AI-made, so the record must show how they were made.

## Consequences
- AI_USAGE.md names the Grok desk.
- **In use:** the figurines and the photos.
- **Not yet used:** the b-roll, the captions, the phone text and the research.
