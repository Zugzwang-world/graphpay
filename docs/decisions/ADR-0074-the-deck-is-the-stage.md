# ADR-0074: The deck is the stage, with the live phones
Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-06 22:30 IST · Decided by: founder · Source: the full-sync brief `docs/thinking-partner/briefs/2225-full-sync.md`; the Booth sessions' #136, #139, #143 and #144

## Decision
- **The stage:** the website's stage is the pitch deck, with the team's two live phones on either side.
- **Navigation:** the phones drive the deck. An errand step or a phone event moves it to its slide. Back and Next only browse.
- **The layout:** one screen, with no QR codes.

## Why
One screen tells the whole story live, and the slide always matches what the phones just did.

## Consequences
- This amends ADR-0070: the middle of the stage is the deck, not T5's explainer.
- T5's other pages stay as they are.
- The deck's non-demo slides have not yet been checked one by one.
