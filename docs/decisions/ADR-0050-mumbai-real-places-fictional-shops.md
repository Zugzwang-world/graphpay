# ADR-0050: Mumbai: real places, fictional shops

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-05 · Decided by: founder (brief v2)

## Decision

The story uses real Mumbai neighbourhoods, dishes and landmarks, and fictional shop names, especially for the trap shop.

## Why

Real places make the demo credible; a real shop must never be shown as the trap or carry invented reputation.

## Consequences

The synthetic city's shops are fictional and labelled synthetic; coordinates are realistic neighbourhood points with their source.
