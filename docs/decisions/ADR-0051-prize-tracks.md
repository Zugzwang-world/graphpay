# ADR-0051: Prize tracks

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-05 · Decided by: founder (brief v2)

## Decision

Arkiv "Build on Arkiv" plus the Swarm bonus; ENS "Best Use of ENSv2"; Whitechain Operate (one Whitechain prize per project). Three partner prizes, the most the submission form allows. GraphPay stays a general Whitechain payments solution with nothing hard-coded to one track.

## Why

These tracks match what the product proves; the founder confirmed the Whitechain one-prize rule.

## Consequences

Extends ADR-0032. Track wording lives only in the sponsor files and the submission text (M17).
