## Geography and evidence cautions

- Matunga provides the densest established Udupi/tiffin references. BKC references include food courts and mixed fast-food counters rather than only traditional standalone tiffin rooms.
- Kalina and Bandra East Mani’s Cafe listings show different addresses; do not assume a shared owner. Their legal identities are UNVERIFIED.
- The BKC Ayyappan entry is present in an area listing, but its exact standalone address/branch identity is UNVERIFIED; do not merge it with the Matunga listing or use it as a confirmed separate branch without another source.
- Udipi Bhuvan/Bhavan in Government Colony, Bandra East appeared in directory research but a current search result marks it shut down. It is excluded from the 25 active-candidate entries above. Historical directory source: [Grotal](https://www.grotal.com/Mumbai/Restaurant-Udipi-C45/); shutdown indicator: [magicpin](https://magicpin.in/Mumbai/Bandra-East/Restaurant/Udipi-Bhuvan/store/2a100).
- Kalanagar now has a concrete historical Tamil-tiffin reference: Dosa Centre opposite Guru Nanak Hospital. A separate NDTV article calls a similar stall Sai Fast Food; identity equivalence is UNVERIFIED, so these are not counted as separate eateries. The 2015 price is explicitly historical, not a current quote.
- Every table row now has a source-reported price or explicitly dated historical price, rather than an invented estimate. Several dish signatures, current operating statuses and the BKC Ayyappan branch identity remain unconfirmed. These gaps must remain visible in the production checklist.
