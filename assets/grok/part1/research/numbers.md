# Published numbers: agent payments and agent usage

Research window: 1 May–6 November 2026. Checked 6 November 2026 (IST). These are published observations, not independent recalculations. Agent registrations, transactions, wallets and downloads are different units and must never be added together. No number below measures GraphPay adoption.

## Source-backed observations

1. **197,157,748 cumulative x402 transactions; approximately $42.5 million settled, across 12 chains.** Source update: 2026-11-06, 09:36 IST. [Agent economy tracker](https://agenteconomy.to/). One-line summary: the tracker publishes cumulative protocol activity, not a count of verified autonomous shoppers. Its embedded examples round volume differently; use approximately $42.5 million, not false dollar-level precision. Classification methodology was not independently audited here.
2. **149.2 million cumulative x402 transactions at May 2026 versus 196.0 million at October 2026.** Source update: 2026-11-06; observation periods: May and October 2026. [Tracker monthly series](https://agenteconomy.to/). One-line summary: cumulative totals rose across these published checkpoints; these are rounded cumulative values, not monthly active users or retained agents.
3. **October 2026: 5.1 million x402 transactions, $274,000 in value.** Publication: 2026-11-03. [Fireblocks monthly comparison](https://www.fireblocks.com/blog/agent-economy-numbers-x402-data). One-line summary: payment count can be large while total economic value is small. Fireblocks cites agenteconomy.to; this is not independent corroboration of the tracker.
4. **Q3 2026: 68.3 million agent payments, $3.7 million in value.** Publication: 2026-11-03. [Fireblocks discussion of Allium](https://www.fireblocks.com/blog/agent-economy-numbers-x402-data). One-line summary: Fireblocks reports Allium's broader classification and says it differs from x402-only trackers. **UNVERIFIED at the original Allium source**; secondary-source context only, not a recommended headline until the original report is checked.
5. **2,053,285 ERC-8004 registered agents across 24 chains.** Source update: 2026-11-06, 09:36 IST. [Agent registry tracker](https://agenteconomy.to/). One-line summary: registrations measure registry entries, not distinct humans, live agents, successful transactions or trustworthy businesses. The same agent may have entries on multiple chains.
6. **More than 50 million agents registered in Microsoft Agent 365.** Publication: 2026-11-03. [Microsoft registration update](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/11/03/microsoft-named-a-leader-in-the-2026-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-ai-agent-management-platforms/). One-line summary: this is a vendor-reported enterprise-management registration figure, not payment activity, daily usage or an independent estimate of unique autonomous agents.

## What the growth evidence does and does not say

The tracker shows rising cumulative activity; cumulative totals cannot fall and do not by themselves show accelerating adoption. Fireblocks' table reports a speculative November 2025 peak of 52.6 million transactions and $20.5 million versus October 2026's 5.1 million and $274,000. Those older observations are outside the requested observation window and are background only, although the reporting date is in-window. Never turn them into an uninterrupted growth claim.

The Microsoft figure supports registration-scale context, not a before/after adoption growth rate. No independently checked, in-window active-agent time series was established here. Downloads, wallet addresses and registrations are not substitutes for active users.

## Numbers not safe to put on a slide as facts

- **UNVERIFIED:** GraphPay's two reads versus about 48, 17 lines, one signature and no-agent-gas flow lack supplied implementation evidence. They are not market statistics.
- **Synthetic:** 4,812 / 1,204 / 2,190 orders, 1.2% / 3.1% / 18.4% refunds and 97% / 93% / 71% on-time figures come from the demo brief, not merchant measurements.
- Forecasts such as trillion-dollar 2030 commerce estimates are not realized payments. No forecast is recommended as the headline in this file.
- Do not use the Fireblocks-repeated Gartner 17% deployment figure without the original survey: the exact population, survey date and question were not checked here.

## Recommended slide wording

“Published trackers report substantial machine-payment activity, but transaction counts do not tell us whether a shop is trustworthy.” Cite the 2026-11-06 tracker beside the number, and label it cumulative protocol activity. Keep the trust argument grounded in what the demo actually implements.
